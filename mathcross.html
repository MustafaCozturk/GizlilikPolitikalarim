🛡️ Zero-Data Retention Statement

As the developer of this application, we do not operate or maintain any external servers, user databases, or user registration mechanisms. We never collect, store, process, sell, or transfer any personal identifiable information (such as name, email, precise location, or device identity). The application operates entirely standalone and serverless.

1. Scope of Responsibility & Data Isolation

The developer, Mustafa Çalışkanöztürk, has no access to and does not mediate any user data. All in-game progress (including solved levels, stars, daily streaks, and user settings) is stored strictly on the user's local physical device storage. No game state or personal data is transmitted to any external server owned by the developer.

2. Third-Party Services & Data Processing

Application operations, advertising, and technical stability features are handled via trusted third-party SDKs. Any telemetry or advertising identifiers processed by these services are transmitted directly between your device and the third-party infrastructure:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC Advertising Service): To keep MathCross free for all users, Google AdMob is utilized for banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google AdMob may process standard anonymous identifiers (such as Apple IDFA or Google Advertising ID) and non-personal diagnostic device information subject to Google's privacy policies.
    Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
    Google Ad Technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Distribution: App delivery, downloads, and platform updates are fulfilled directly through Apple and Google ecosystems. No billing or personal account details are accessible to the developer.

3. Data Breach & Security Exemption

Because no user data, accounts, or cloud databases exist on the developer's infrastructure, developer-side data leakage or privacy breaches are technically impossible. All third-party communication adheres strictly to the security certifications and privacy frameworks established by Google LLC.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

MathCross is an all-ages educational and logic math puzzle. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the age threshold required by applicable jurisdictions). Advertisements delivered comply with applicable child protection filters.

5. Data Deletion & User Control

Because all progress is stored locally on your device, you can completely erase your data at any time by clearing application data in device settings or simply uninstalling the application.
